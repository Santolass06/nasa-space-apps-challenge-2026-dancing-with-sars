# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are a hackathon team building an open, science-driven project. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the team and the project
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull
requests, chat) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported
to the repository maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and
will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
